Summary of multimedia objects
=============================

b1_sarcasm_triplets/context_triplets_en.json
  - 200 contextual sarcasm triplets (S / P / N) used in
    Experiment B (valence stress test).
  - Each item: intent, keywords, sarcastic, positive, negative
    texts; aligned with iSarcasmEval SubTask C English test
    after human rewrite.
  - Usage: reproduce sentence-level and keyword-level valence
    projections reported in the paper.

b1_sarcasm_triplets/rewrite_protocol.md
  - Human rewrite protocol for building the 200 triplets from
    the original sarcasm–rephrase pairs (de-culturation, shared
    intent, S/P identical quote, keyword annotation).
  - Usage: understand how the released JSON was constructed;
    not required to read the main PDF results.

Value
-----
Enables independent re-running of Experiment B projections on
the exact stimuli used in the paper, without requesting data
from the authors during review.
