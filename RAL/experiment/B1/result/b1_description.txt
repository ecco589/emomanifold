B1 Experiment: Contextual Sarcasm Stress Test (iSarcasmEval SubTask C)
======================================================================

Purpose
-------
Test whether the token-level valence readout captures pragmatic intent beyond
surface-positive wording. Using sarcasm–rephrase pairs, we ask: when a sentence
uses positive words but means something negative, does projected valence align
with the literal-negative rephrase rather than with the positive surface?

Setup
-----
- Model: Qwen2.5-0.5B (forward-only, no fine-tuning)
- Emotion axes + AdaptiveScaler: E1 en/pole_50 (pole_n=50)
- Source data: iSarcasmEval SubTask C English test set (200 pairs)
- Rewritten dataset: RAL/experiment/B1/data/context_triplets_en.json
  (200 triplets × sarcastic / sincere-positive / literal-negative)
- Rewrite protocol: RAL/experiment/B1/data/rewrite_protocol.md (6-step manual)
- Sentence-level V: last-token projection after scaler
- Keyword-level V/A/D: token at anchored emotion keyword(s)

Rewrite summary
---------------
Each original pair was manually rewritten into three conditions sharing intent:
  S (sarcastic)  — positive surface words, negative intent, bad situational trigger
  P (positive)   — positive surface words, positive intent, good situational trigger
  N (negative)   — negative surface words, negative intent, bad situational trigger
Culture-specific names (politicians, sports teams, brands, etc.) were replaced
with generic everyday settings (shop, office, home, clinic, …).

Results — sentence-level valence (n=200 triplets)
--------------------------------------------------
  Mean V (sarcastic)  = 0.463
  Mean V (positive)   = 0.654
  Mean V (negative)  = 0.403

  Mean Δ(S − N)       = +0.060   (sarcastic vs literal-negative; near overlap)
  Mean Δ(S − P)       = −0.191   (sarcastic below sincere-positive)

  |Δ(S − N)| < 0.15   = 81.5% of triplets (163/200)
  Both S and N < 0    = 0.0%

Results — keyword token-level (anchored emotion words)
------------------------------------------------------
  Primary keyword per triplet (n=200):
    Mean ΔV(S − P) = −0.216

  All keyword rows with both S and P hits (n=325):
    Valence:   mean Δ(S − P) = −0.204   mean Δ(S − N) = +0.090
    Arousal:   mean Δ(S − P) = +0.070   mean Δ(S − N) = −0.007
    Dominance: mean Δ(S − P) = −0.071   mean Δ(S − N) = +0.005

Comparison — bare vs contextual (development)
---------------------------------------------
  Bare iSarcasmEval (10-pair smoke, no context):
    Mean V_sarc ≈ 0.416, Mean V_reph ≈ 0.008, Mean Δ(S−N) ≈ +0.408
  Contextual rewrite (full 200 triplets):
    Mean Δ(S−N) ≈ +0.060, keyword ΔV(S−P) ≈ −0.20

Interpretation (short)
----------------------
Without context, sarcastic sentences stay strongly positive at sentence level.
After contextual rewrite, sarcastic and literal-negative sentence valences largely
overlap (81.5% within ±0.15). At the same positive keyword, sarcastic context
yields systematically lower valence than sincere-positive context (Δ ≈ −0.20),
supporting context-sensitive readout rather than pure lexical lookup.
