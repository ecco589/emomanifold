**Closed-loop execution of token-level affect under a four-condition, same-session comparison (n = 20 utterances).**
**(A)** Direction agreement between commanded affect transitions (2 s window) and camera readback. Ours: valence 87% (95% CI [77, 96]), arousal 70% (CI [57, 82]); all baselines at chance. Bars: trial-mean accuracy with trial-clustered bootstrap 95% CI; dots: per-trial accuracy; dashed line: chance (50%).
**(B)** Representative trial 005: fixed-lag-aligned, z-scored commanded vs executed trajectories.
**(C)** Facial variance explained beyond a turn-level constant expression. Token-level control adds +22 / +19 pp (valence/arousal); turn-level and shuffled baselines add none by construction.
All conditions share one fixed execution+detection lag (0.5 s); FER readback linearly calibrated on the static-face conditions (calibration set independent of the tested dynamics).
